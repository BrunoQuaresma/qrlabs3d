## Business overview

- **Name:** QRLabs 3D.
- **Location:** Recife, Pernambuco, Brazil.
- **Focus:** 3D-printed objects, with an emphasis on custom pieces and designs.
- **Stage:** A newly established business with a small portfolio.
- **Business goals:** Attract more customers, win custom projects, and grow the portfolio through completed work.

These facts were provided by the business owner. A small portfolio reflects the business's current stage; the website should make the available work compelling without implying an extensive track record.

## Website purpose

The website's primary job is to turn interest in a custom 3D-printed piece into a WhatsApp conversation about a project quote.

The intended visitor journey is:

1. Understand what QRLabs 3D does.
2. Explore real examples of its work and customization possibilities.
3. Start a WhatsApp conversation to discuss an idea and request a quote.

The confirmed conversion goal is a conversation, rather than an online purchase. On October 31, the owner set the SEO priority: attract people in Recife looking for custom and personalized items that can be made with a 3D printer. The current portfolio supports examples of decoration, miniatures/presents, and branded displays; the complete product and service offering is still to be defined.

## Suggested website direction

The following are working recommendations, not additional confirmed business facts:

- Use Brazilian Portuguese for customer-facing content.
- Adopt a friendly, creative, approachable tone, with clear explanations for people unfamiliar with 3D printing.
- Emphasize customization and an invitation to discuss an idea.
- Give Recife a clear place in the business introduction. Being based in Recife does not establish a delivery or shipping area.
- Show a small, carefully selected portfolio of genuine work. Add new projects as they are completed.
- Use actual project and process photos when available. Clearly distinguish concepts or illustrative renders from completed customer work.
- Avoid invented testimonials, customer counts, years of experience, or claims about materials, precision, pricing, turnaround, or shipping.

Possible introductory copy, pending owner review:

> Impressão 3D personalizada em Recife. Conte sua ideia para a QRLabs 3D e converse com a gente sobre o seu projeto.

## Details to clarify as the website develops

- Which customers and kinds of projects should the website prioritize?
- Does the business create 3D models from scratch, adapt existing designs, print customer-supplied files, or offer all three?
- Which materials, sizes, colors, painting options, and finishes are available?
- Is fulfillment local pickup, delivery in Recife, shipping elsewhere, or a combination?
- What information is needed for a quote, and are there minimum orders or typical lead times?
- Which completed projects and photos should be featured first?

Until confirmed, these details should not be presented as promises or established services.
